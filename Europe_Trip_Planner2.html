Lucerne & Swiss Alps
Mt. Pilatus Chapel Bridge Trümmelbach Falls

Welcome to Rick Steves' #1 "wow moment" in Europe! The Swiss Alps deliver breathtaking scenery, thrilling mountain railways, and pristine lakes. Kids adore the cog trains, cable cars, and snow activities. Lucerne's charming Old Town and iconic Chapel Bridge complete the Alpine dream.

Expert Advice

Final Notes & Travel Tips

directions_walkPacing Strategy

Follow the proven formula: mornings for active sightseeing, afternoons for parks and rest. Kids (and parents!) need downtime. A tired, overstimulated child makes for miserable travel. Rest days are non-negotiable.

luggageLuggage

With 6 cities in 22 days, pack light. Use 1 medium checked bag + 1 carry-on per person. Duffel bags are easier than roller bags for multiple moves.

photo_cameraPhotography & Memories

Encourage kids to keep a travel journal with drawings and stickers. Take one family photo per day. These simple habits create lifelong memories — don't get so focused on photos that you miss the moment.

securitySafety

Know your hotel's address in the local language. Save emergency embassy contact numbers. Teach kids your phone number. Use hotel business cards to find your way back. Trust your instincts.

paymentsCurrency & Payments

EU countries use Euro. UK uses Pounds Sterling. Switzerland uses Swiss Francs. Use ATMs for cash; most places accept credit cards. Notify your bank of travel dates to avoid card blocks.

wb_sunnyBest Time to Visit

June is ideal: warm but not scorching, fewer crowds than July/August, longer daylight hours. Book major attractions 4–6 weeks ahead to secure tickets and time slots.
